## Purpose

Support library names, controlled generation and learner progress.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Lasting self-assessment
Users SHALL rate each flipped card correct/incorrect, resume incomplete rounds and see completion results and history. Progress SHALL be isolated per user and content revision.

#### Scenario: Finish and repeat a deck
- **WHEN** all cards have a rating
- **THEN** the platform stores the score, shows the result and allows a new round while retaining earlier results
